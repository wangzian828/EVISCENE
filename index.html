THE QUESTION

Verify the situation.
Not just the image’s origin.

A genuine photograph can be misleading. A generated image can depict a factually consistent scene. Origin and factuality are different questions.

INPUT

An image

Identify the people, objects, events, text, and context that can be checked.

INVESTIGATION

Evidence, not appearance

Find sources and inspect whether their details actually match the depicted situation.

OUTPUT

A grounded assessment

Supported Refuted

With an explanation tied to the evidence.

View the task definition from the paper

THE EVISCENE AGENT

Look closer.
Follow the evidence.

A multimodal ReAct policy chooses what to inspect, where to search, and when the evidence is sufficient to conclude.

01 OBSERVE

Inspect the image

Read the scene and its details. Surface specific, checkable cues rather than relying on visual plausibility.

Scene perception · Focused inspection
02 RETRIEVE

Find the context

Search the web and retrieve related images to identify relevant events, entities, and sources.

Web search · Image retrieval
03 VERIFY

Check the match

Visit source pages and compare reference images. Test dates, identities, and details against the input.

Page visits · Reference comparison
04 DECIDE

Revisit and conclude

Resolve open questions, then produce a verdict supported by the collected evidence.

Reasoning · Evidence-grounded verdict

↺ An investigation loop, not a fixed checklist. The policy can revisit observations as new evidence arrives.
